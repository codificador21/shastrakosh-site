Privacy policy

Effective date: 18 November 2026
Contact: support@shastrakosh.in

Shastra Kosh ("the app") lets you read Hindu scriptures. We collect as little as possible.

What stays on your phone

Everything you read, the verses you save, your reading progress, your streak and your settings are stored on your device. If you never create an account, none of this leaves your phone.

If you create an account

To sync across devices we store, in our database hosted by Supabase in Mumbai, India:

  • your email address, used to sign you in with a one-time code;
  • your saved verses, reading progress and reading streak;
  • which one-time purchases you own (for example "Mahabharata" or "Everything"), so they unlock on your other devices.

We use this data only to provide the app. We do not sell it, show ads, or use advertising or tracking SDKs.

Purchases

Purchases are made through the App Store or Google Play, which handle all payment details; we never see your card or bank information. To confirm what you own, the app uses RevenueCat, a purchase-verification service. RevenueCat receives the store's purchase record (product, date, store transaction id) and a random app user id, or your account id if you are signed in. It does not receive your name or payment details. RevenueCat's privacy policy: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy

Notifications

The optional Verse of the Day reminder is scheduled on your phone. It does not send data to us.

Your choices and rights

You can sign out at any time. You can delete your account in Account → Delete account, which permanently removes your account and all synced data from our servers. You can also contact us at support@shastrakosh.in to access, correct or delete your data, as provided by India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and other applicable laws.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13; accounts are optional.

Changes

We will post changes here and update the effective date.
