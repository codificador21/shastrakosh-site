Support

Write to support@shastrakosh.in. We usually reply within two working days.

Restore a purchase

Open Settings → Unlock scriptures → Restore purchases, signed in to the same Apple ID or Google account you bought with.

Refunds

Purchases are handled by the store. Request a refund from Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com, or from Google Play under Payments & subscriptions → Budget & history.

Delete your account

In the app, open Settings → Account → Delete account. This permanently removes your account and synced data from our servers. You can also email us to ask for deletion.

Reset reading progress

Settings → Reset reading progress starts every text again from the first verse. Saved verses are kept.

A translation looks wrong

Translations are historical public-domain works. If a verse looks misaligned or garbled, email us the reference shown at the bottom of the card, for example "Mahabharata · 12.132.14".
