Shastra Koshशास्त्र कोश

The Gita, Ramayana, Mahabharata and the Vedas, one verse at a time

Shastra Kosh is a calm reader for India's great scriptures: Sanskrit in Devanagari over classical paintings, with public-domain English and Hindi translations. The Bhagavad Gita is free. Every translation is the work of a named scholar; nothing is written by AI.
